Search improved each narrow condition by only one paired episode, so the committed evidence supports bounded non-regression and two concrete rescues, not a statistically significant search advantage.

Explicit pose-estimator transfer evidence was generated by [GitHub Actions run 45056792299](https://github.com/haoyu020206-netizen/panda-contact-assembly/actions/runs/45056792299) from commit `bf0d60a`:

- `pose-estimator-transfer/pose_estimator_aggregate.json`: six sensing conditions, three seeds, two policies, estimator telemetry, and paired rescue/harm counts;
- `pose-estimator-transfer/pose_estimator_runs.csv`: compact per-seed condition/policy results;
- `pose-estimator-transfer/pose_estimator_aggregate.png`: success, safety, estimate-age, and observed-dropout comparison;
- `pose-estimator-transfer/seed-results/`: all 720 episode records plus the 36 per-policy and three per-seed summaries;
- `pose-estimator-transfer/provenance.json`: source commit, workflow inputs, artifacts, and formal acceptance thresholds.

The three PyTorch source checkpoints remain in the workflow artifacts rather than Git. Strict CI validation requires three seeds, 20 episodes per condition/policy/seed, at least 75% ideal-pose learned success, at least 70% combined-condition learned success, at least 60% learned success in every condition, no more than six learned safety terminations in any condition, and exact agreement between seed summaries and every episode CSV. Search non-regression is deliberately not required: the paired evidence shows 14 harmed trials under 120 ms latency and 16 under the combined condition, exposing stale-pose entry detection as the next control defect. The estimator consumes simulator-derived measurements, so this evidence tests temporal/error robustness rather than real camera perception.

Full per-step time-series CSV files remain downloadable from the workflow artifact. The repository does not claim that these lightweight-model forces transfer directly to hardware; they are simulation metrics for controller comparison and regression testing.
